Data science and analytics projects

COVID-19 mortality risk

Healthcare machine learning · 3.9 million patients, Mexico

A model that scores each confirmed patient's 30-day risk of death at first attendance, so a health unit can decide who to monitor closely and who needs a bed. Trained on 2020 and tested once on 2021.

ROC-AUC 0.949 on 2.4 million later patients; the 10% it flags first include 78% of deaths, and its risks are calibrated (5.8% predicted, 5.7% observed).

Gradient boosting, 15-model screen, temporal and state-held-out validation, calibration, SHAP

Cardiovascular risk against the clinical standard

Clinical analytics and survival analysis · 23,888 US adults

A 10-year cardiovascular mortality model built on NHANES linked to the National Death Index, compared with the Pooled Cohort Equations used in clinical guidelines.

ROC-AUC 0.822 against 0.799 on a later period, and risk numbers that mean what they say: observed/expected 1.01, where the guideline equations overstate risk about fourfold.

18-model screen incl. XGBoost and LightGBM, temporal validation, Cox survival model (C-index 0.807), calibration and decision curves, SHAP

Retail demand forecasting and inventory

Time series and decision analytics · 2,283 daily and hourly series

Forecasts tied to three planning decisions: weekly orders per product and outlet, store replenishment and hourly staffing, each judged on the decision rather than on forecast error alone.

10% less stock than the best rule of thumb for the same 95% fill rate, and a staffing plan 7% cheaper than the best rule-based plan.

Quantile gradient boosting, benchmarked against ARIMA, SARIMA, Holt-Winters, Prophet and Croston/SBA/TSB; time-ordered validation, Diebold-Mariano tests, SHAP

Smart-home appliance anomaly detection

IoT sensor data · 129 million readings from 20 UK homes

An anomaly detector for how household appliances are functioning, built on smart-plug power sensors and validated on fridges and freezers in homes it never saw.

86% of annotated anomalies caught at under one false alert per appliance a month, against 78% for a timer rule, with alerts about 48 minutes earlier.

Gradient boosting, Isolation Forest and rule baselines, leave-houses-out validation, event-level scoring, SHAP

Hotel review sentiment

NLP and customer analytics · 515,000 reviews

A text classifier that flags unhappy guests in free-text reviews, so a guest-relations team knows which to answer first.

Reading 7.2% of reviews in the model's order reaches 81% of unhappy guests, on 299 hotels it never saw (PR-AUC 0.849).

TF-IDF and logistic regression, best of 11 model families, hotel-grouped validation, calibration, SHAP

Brain-tumour MRI classification

Deep learning and explainable AI · 3,064 scans, 233 patients

A transfer-learning classifier that identifies tumour type from one MRI slice, as a second-reader aid, validated on patients it never saw.

90.1% balanced accuracy and 0.983 macro AUC; along the way, an audit showed 54% of a popular benchmark's test images are duplicated in its training set.

ResNet50 and five other architectures in TensorFlow/Keras, patient-grouped validation, robustness tests, Grad-CAM and SHAP checked against tumour outlines
